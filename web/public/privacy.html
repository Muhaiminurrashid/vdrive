Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account: email address, display name, and any files you upload. We also collect basic usage data such as page views and feature interactions to improve the service.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate and improve Virtual Pendrive: authenticate you, store and retrieve your files, generate access codes, and provide customer support. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Auth, Firestore) for authentication and data storage, and Backblaze B2 for file storage via a Cloudflare Worker proxy. These services have their own privacy policies and are GDPR-compliant.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Data Retention

You can delete your files or account at any time. Data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days of deletion.
